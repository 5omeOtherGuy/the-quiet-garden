The Quiet Garden

For grown-ups: nothing to win, nothing to finish. Touch anywhere to go in, then touch the world: stroke it, tap it, rest a finger. Circle a finger for wind, tap a cloud for rain (hold it for a storm), rub with two to wipe the sky clear, rest a whole hand for mist, pull the sun down for night, rest a finger on the sun for the next season (or twist two fingers).
